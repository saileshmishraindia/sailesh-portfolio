A natural extension of My Foundation

Learn the principle. Demonstrate the practice.

The academy extends the Foundation's discipline of understanding deeply, testing honestly and explaining clearly into proposed, structured technical learning pathways.

Fundamentals with a purpose

Begin with physical principles, circuit behaviour and computational thinking. Use simulations and small experiments to connect mathematical models to observable results before progressing to larger engineering systems.

Competence supported by evidence

Proposed pathways combine a published syllabus, practical exercises, project documentation and assessment. Industry-oriented problems guide learning; no employment, placement or professional licensing is guaranteed.

Current stage: curriculum planning. Departments and catalogue entries are proposed. No course enrolment, payment, student account or certificate issuance service is active on this site. Existing class notes and the simulation laboratory remain available as Foundation resources, not as evidence of course completion.

Learning methodology

A progression, not a shortcut.

Each stage builds evidence for the next. Certification is conditional on published requirements, not simply access to learning material.

  1. 01Understand

    Study fundamentals, assumptions and governing equations.

  2. 02Explore

    Inspect examples, simulate alternatives and ask what changes.

  3. 03Implement

    Build a circuit, script or design and document its behaviour.

  4. 04Evaluate

    Test results against a rubric and explain limitations.

  5. 05Certify

    Receive an independent credential only when the course's stated criteria are met.

Transparent independent credentials

Say precisely what was demonstrated.

The proposed framework distinguishes participation from completion and achievement. Final requirements need academic approval and will be published for each course before enrolment.

Participation

Records qualifying attendance and participation. It does not establish assessed competence or a passing grade.

Course Completion

Records completion of the prescribed learning activities, assignments and applicable assessments.

Achievement

Records assessed performance against a course's published achievement standard, including practical or project evidence.

Before you begin

Academy questions

Can I enrol in a course today?

No. The catalogue currently describes proposed pathways marked Coming Soon or In Development. Availability will only change after the syllabus, delivery arrangements, assessment rules and relevant policies are finalised.

Is this different from the professional portfolio?

Yes. The personal portfolio remains the professional engineering identity. My Foundation explains the underlying learning practice; the academy is its structured technical education extension.

Does a certificate confer a degree or guarantee employment?

No. These are proposed independent training credentials. They do not confer a degree, statutory qualification, licence, placement or employment guarantee.

What can I use while courses are being developed?

Explore the existing annotated notes and simulation projects. Reading or downloading these resources does not constitute enrolment or a certificate award.
