Admissions information / planning stage

Begin with clear expectations.

Understand the proposed entry requirements, learning format and assessment responsibilities before committing to a course.

No admissions, registration, waitlist or payment service is active. This page provides planning information, not an offer of a course place.

Who may apply when admissions open?

A pathway matched to your starting point.

The proposed audience includes engineering students, diploma learners, graduates, working professionals and self-directed learners. Each course will publish its own eligibility requirements; interest alone does not replace technical prerequisites.

Academic readiness

Foundation outlines generally assume school-level mathematics and basic computer literacy. Intermediate outlines require the relevant prior circuit, digital-design or programming knowledge. No blanket degree requirement is being announced.

Technical prerequisites

Plan for a suitable computer, internet access and legal access to any required tools. Specific operating systems, tool versions, hardware and software costs will be disclosed before enrolment. Do not purchase tools based on a draft outline.

Learners below 18

No minor enrolment is enabled. Before accepting learners below 18, age-appropriate notices, verifiable parental or lawful guardian consent where required, and child-safety safeguards must be implemented and reviewed for applicable Indian law.

Proposed admission process

Review first. Enrol only after launch.

These steps describe the intended process. They are not a live application workflow, and no eligibility or place is confirmed through this page.

Planned online learning format

Participation means doing the work.

The intended format combines guided explanations, self-study, simulations, programming or design tasks, and project documentation. Whether a course includes live sessions, recordings or scheduled reviews remains to be announced.

Learning expectations

Study prerequisites, attempt exercises independently, document assumptions, retain simulation or test evidence, cite sources and seek clarification when results disagree with theory. Use only authorised tools and non-confidential data.

Attendance & participation

There is no active attendance policy or fixed percentage today. Before enrolment, each course must state how attendance or equivalent participation is recorded, what is mandatory, and how missed activities or accessibility needs are handled.

Assessment requirements

Planned assessments may include theory questions, practical assignments, projects and a final assessment. Final rubrics, pass rules, deadlines, resubmission options and any AI-use limits must be published before enrolment.

Policy pending / no payment service

Fees, cancellations & refunds

No course fees, discounts, batch dates or payment instructions have been established. Do not send money or payment credentials in response to this site.

Required before any paid enrolment

  • A confirmed provider identity, published contact details and any applicable invoicing or tax information.
  • A complete course price, applicable taxes, included services and separately required software or hardware costs.
  • Cancellation, withdrawal, refund eligibility, deductions if any, refund process and timelines.
  • Provider cancellation or postponement terms and a defined grievance/escalation channel.
  • A vetted payment provider and lawful transaction handling, with no card or banking details collected by this static site.

These are policy topics awaiting approval, not current refund promises or binding operational terms.

Student support

Ask about the pathway, not a promised place.

General academy enquiries can use the owner's existing public email channel. There is no helpdesk, response-time commitment, instructor allocation or active student-support service yet.

General academy enquiry
Keep enquiries minimal.

Ask a course or accessibility question without sending Aadhaar, identity documents, birth dates, marksheets, passwords, payment information or private student records. Parents or guardians should make general enquiries on behalf of children without sending child-identifying information. Email uses your email provider and is not an admissions form.

Provisional privacy notice / launch requirements

Collect less. Protect what is necessary.

No academy registration form, payment integration, student account, analytics tracker or certificate-record database has been added. Certificate IDs entered in the current interface are processed locally, are not stored by the academy script and are not submitted to a registry.

Current static-site boundaries

GitHub Pages and existing font/icon providers may process connection information such as IP addresses when assets load, under their own policies. External links and mail applications have their own data practices. This academy script does not add cookies, local storage or tracking.

Before student data is collected

Publish the responsible entity and privacy contact, lawful purposes and notices, data categories, consent and withdrawal arrangements where applicable, retention/deletion rules, rights-request routes, security safeguards, processors, transfers and incident handling. Review obligations under applicable Indian data-protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection framework and rules in force at launch.

Children require additional safeguards. Before any under-18 registration, obtain legal review of age assurance, verifiable guardian consent where required, child-appropriate communications, access restrictions and limitations on tracking or behavioural monitoring. Do not upload student work containing third-party personal data or confidential employer material. This notice is not a claim that a future service is already legally compliant.
Provisional terms / not an enrolment agreement

What this site does and does not offer.

  • Draft course outlines and estimates may change. They do not reserve a place, establish a delivery date or promise a certificate.
  • Do not represent example material or the specimen certificate as an earned credential.
  • Observe source attribution, tool licensing and applicable rights when using learning resources. No additional licence to redistribute existing material is granted here.
  • No degree, statutory recognition, employment, placement, professional licensing or third-party endorsement is promised.
  • Before enrolment, publish approved learner conduct, academic integrity, permitted AI use, accessibility, complaints, dispute handling and cancellation terms, together with the provider's legally required disclosures.

The provider identity, operative terms, jurisdiction and grievance contact require owner confirmation and appropriate legal review before commercial operation.

Admissions questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I submit an application or join a waitlist?

Not yet. There is no application or waitlist form. Check the catalogue for planning information; no enrolment date has been announced.

Are courses free or paid?

This has not been established. Course-specific fees, inclusions and refund terms must be published before any enrolment or payment is accepted.

Will lessons be live or recorded?

The delivery mix is not confirmed. Each course must publish its format, platform, access period, schedule if applicable and support arrangements before enrolment.

Is hardware required?

Requirements will vary by course. Draft practical work emphasises simulation and software exercises. Do not buy hardware or commercial licences until the approved course requirements are published.

Is there a certificate for simply viewing the resources?

No. Resource access is not enrolment, completion or a credential award. See the independent certification framework for the proposed distinctions.
