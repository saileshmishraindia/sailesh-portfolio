Legal and academic transparency

Independent training. Clearly described.

VLSITech Technical Learning Academy is an independent private technical education initiative under My Foundation. Its proposed certificates are independently issued professional training credentials.

Not a degree or statutory qualification.

Academy certificates do not constitute university degrees, UGC-recognised qualifications or government-recognised vocational qualifications. No UGC, AICTE, NCVET, government, university or other statutory recognition, accreditation, affiliation or authorisation is claimed. They do not confer professional licensing or guarantee employment or placement.

This is a proposed framework requiring final academic approval, not an active certificate-awarding service. Course-specific requirements, rubrics and credential wording must be published before enrolment and must not be changed retrospectively without a fair, clearly communicated process.

Three proposed categories

What does each certificate mean?

Participation

Certificate of Participation

Records qualifying attendance and participation in the specified learning activity. It is not a statement of mastery or a passing examination result.

  • Attendance or equivalent participation evidence must meet the course's published minimum.
  • Required participation activities must be completed.
  • No assessed passing result is implied unless explicitly and separately stated.
Completion

Certificate of Course Completion

Records completion of the prescribed syllabus activities and required submissions, with any completion-level assessment rules satisfied.

  • Meet the course's participation requirement.
  • Complete all mandatory assignments and applicable examinations.
  • Submit the required project, where included, and meet the published completion criteria.
Achievement

Certificate of Achievement

Records demonstrated performance against an explicit assessed standard, supported by examination, practical and project evidence.

  • Meet the course's completion requirements.
  • Pass the approved assessment threshold and any mandatory component minimums.
  • Meet the project rubric and academic-integrity requirements.

Attendance and assignments

No universal attendance percentage is approved. Every course must state attendance or equivalent engagement requirements, which assignments are mandatory, submission deadlines, late-work handling, accessibility arrangements and resubmission rules before enrolment.

Examinations and project evaluation

Publish exam format, permitted resources, AI-use rules, any identity-check method, project rubric, assessor responsibilities, component pass marks and review or appeal procedure. Projects should be judged on correctness, reasoning, testing and reproducible evidence, not presentation alone.

Proposed credential lifecycle

Issue carefully. Correct transparently.

These are design requirements for a future approved process. No issuance, reissuance, review timeline or authorised signatory has been established on this static site.

Issuance

Confirm the learner's eligibility, attendance and final assessment evidence; complete academic review; approve the credential category; then assign a unique, non-predictable certificate ID, issue date and authorised signatory. Preserve an access-controlled audit record. Never issue a credential solely because a fee was paid.

Correction and reissuance

A future secure request process should allow errors to be reviewed using proportionate evidence. Corrections must retain an audit trail, identify the replacement and mark the former credential as superseded where appropriate. Required evidence, any charges and service timelines must be published in advance, not invented here.

Misconduct, revocation and review

Proven fraud, impersonation, falsified evidence or serious academic misconduct may justify revocation under published rules. Provide notice, an opportunity to respond, documented review, a reasoned decision and an appeal route. Do not publish private allegations or unnecessary personal data in verification results.

Verification

Connect certificate IDs to a trusted backend or properly controlled published registry. Public results should disclose only the minimum credential information and current valid, revoked or superseded status. Today, the interface is not connected and cannot establish authenticity.
