Driving innovation in semiconductor technologies

Being a CAD Engineer with a strong foundation in Microelectronics and VLSI, backed by hands-on experience across full-custom design, verification, IoT systems, and design automation. My work is centered on translating engineering concepts into practical semiconductor solutions that are reliable, scalable, and industry-relevant.
